Short bio · ~50 words

Eric Martin is a founding member of Technotronic and co-writer of the 14M-selling Pump Up The Jam, The Album. Three decades of producing across house, hip-hop and soul. Today: owner of Musicated Records, touring the official Technotronic DJ Set worldwide, and releasing as Me One — the modern melodic MC.

Long bio · ~280 words

Eric started as a writer/producer at Zomba/Jive Records, then came to fame as a founding member of the seminal '90s house act Technotronic, co-writing their classic Pump Up The Jam, The Album — over 14 million sales worldwide in its first year.

His first solo project, As Far As I'm Concerned, landed on Island Records with features from Group Home, Tony Touch, Evil Dee and The Roots — and was shortlisted for the Mercury Music Prize.

Production credits include Maxi Priest, Akil The MC (Jurassic 5), Capleton, Shaun Escoffery and Jeff Beck. Today he tours the official, ground-breaking Eric Martin / Technotronic DJ Set globally, runs Musicated Records (multiple Traxsource Garage #1s), and operates the label's TV / Film Department — currently placing the catalogue across VH1, E!, NBA TV, MTV and Hollyoaks.

In 2024 he received the Welsh Music Prize — Inspiration Award for his services to Technotronic's Pump Up The Jam album. In 2026 he signed a new artist deal with Universal. The next Me One release, Phenomenal, follows.
