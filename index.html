02 / ME ONE

A PIONEERING MELODIC MC

Acoustic guitars blend soulful Hip-Hop with Jazz. A unique voice of the melodic MC movement. Me One's As Far As I'm Concerned LP was the UK's first melodic rap album. Released in 1999. Undoubtedly influential. The Hey, Listen This EP is out now on Bandcamp. Phenomenal follows next.
